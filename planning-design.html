WHY WE DESIGNED IT THIS WAY

Design Decisions

01

Colour Selection

Warm ivory, dark green and gold create a welcoming, professional visual identity.

02

Typography

We used Montserrat for headings and Roboto for normal text to make everything clear and easy to read.

03

Navigation

We used the same navigation links on all pages to help users move easily between the seven pages.

04

Responsive Design

We used flexible layouts to make the website look good on computers, tablets, and phones.

05

Consistency

We used the same colours, buttons, cards, and footers across all pages to keep the website looking similar.
