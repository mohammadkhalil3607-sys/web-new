Project Reflection & AI Evaluation

Our development journey and learning experience

1. Project Reflection and Goals

Coaching Hub is a website that allows people to access coaching services and education relating to training and nutrition. It contains tools that calculate relevant fitness information and provides details on nutritional products. We wanted to create a user friendly, interactive and adaptable site incorporating HTML, CSS and JavaScript, aimed at sportspeople, new exercisers and those who wish to control their weight.

2. Development and Technical Challenges

We were given a reference design to show us the general style which should be implemented. The original color scheme, hero and card designs, however, had not fulfilled our vision, so we redesigned the website using the given reference and assessment criteria.

CSS Grid was used for card and page layout and Flexbox was used to center the navigation bar. One person developed CSS templates and color schemes, decreasing repetition and ensuring that all pages followed the same style.

At times, team members attempted to upload work to GitHub at the same time and therefore Subtopic 1 was overwritten. By analyzing the Git history, we were able to identify and fix the incorrect code. These challenges enhanced our knowledge of responsive, styled and version controlled websites.

3. AI Utilization and Critical Evaluation

During the project, AI was utilized. Chat GPT recommended us to change the dark theme to something lighter and more user friendly. AI was also used to generate images and logos together with assisting our understanding of mathematics on the Nutrition page.

Occasionally, AI provided hyperlinks and image formats. It also suggested unsuitable designs for the Reflection page border meaning we had to reflect back to the original designs.

Claude Code was utilized to analyze the menu and footer on our Home page. This then allowed team members to implement standardized page templates.

Grok was used to highlight and fix the inconsistencies in our code to ensure that all pages are all aligned and consistent.

Although AI can assist in editing and executing the project, outputs must be analyzed. A Good use of an AI includes analyzing its work, recording its outputs and citing sources. plagiarism should also be avoided. Ideally AI will enhance human knowledge and decision making, rather than replace it.

4. Team Collaboration Experience

Within the group work, Ali Basheer was responsible for producing the Homepage, Subtopic 1 and Overview. He also produced standard CSS and a resources bank.

Mohammed Khalil redesigned the core website, and developed Subtopic 2. He was also involved in altering the Reflection and Assisted in the Overview sections. Yousef managed the Training and Planning and Design areas. Mohammed Jassim developed the initial Reflection & AI Report.

The group leader organized each person’s output. Even though specializing in different areas was beneficial to team working, implementing a greater level of organization and regularly holding review meetings would have further improved our effectiveness.

5. Lessons Learned and Future Improvements

We have developed our understanding of HTML, CSS and linear gradients. We also furthered our knowledge of JavaScript and applied this to introduce both mathematical and conditional functions which has improved the interactive quality of our site.

We also Implemented AI within our coding process for both coding and analysis and learnt how useful collaboration can be through GitHub. Again, testing was highlighted as an integral part of the site development and is relevant to the assessment criteria.

In future, we will define further the stages of our project. We will continually review the assessment criteria and will produce separate Git branches so that problems can be avoided. Moreover, we will spend further time ensuring that our site is responsive. This will enchase collaboration and help to achieve higher levels of consistency and reliability.
